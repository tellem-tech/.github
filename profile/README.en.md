We build **Tellem** to help businesses understand their customers and encourage **repeat sales**. The CRM brings together customer profiles, marketing, loyalty and analytics. The customer app lets customers place orders in a browser or, with a connected bot, in Telegram.

## 🧩 What Tellem can do

| Goal | Features |
| --- | --- |
| **Understand your customers** | Customer profiles with order history and loyalty point balances. [RFM analysis](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/analytics/) groups customers by how recently they ordered, how often they buy and how much they spend. |
| **Bring customers back** | Send [campaigns](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/mass-mailings/) immediately or on a schedule for one location — to all subscribed customers or selected RFM groups. Telegram, VK and email are available when the channel is connected and the customer has given permission. |
| **Build loyalty** | [Promotions, promo codes and loyalty points](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/marketing/) for future purchases. |
| **Measure results** | [Analytics](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/analytics/) for sales, repeat purchases and customer retention, including cohort reports. |
| **Accept orders** | A [customer app](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/app/) with a catalog, cart and payment. It works in a browser or inside Telegram when a bot is connected. |
| **Connect your services** | Connect [payment providers](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/payment-providers/), [Telegram and VK](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/integrations/), and [iiko](https://docs.tellem.tech/en/modules/crm/iiko/) for sending orders to your POS. |
